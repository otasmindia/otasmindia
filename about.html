WHO WE ARE

Driving innovation through embedded engineering.

OTASM Innovations and Services LLP is an engineering and technology solutions company focused on embedded product development for industrial and commercial applications.

Our technical capabilities cover Sensor Technologies, Motor Control, Industrial Automation, IoT, Industrial Connectivity, Edge AI, RTOS, and Embedded Linux. We bring these disciplines together to support the development of intelligent, connected and reliable products.

We believe successful product engineering begins with a clear understanding of the application. We work to translate requirements into practical system solutions, balancing performance, reliability, integration and long-term maintainability.

Our goal is to be a dependable engineering partner—supporting customers as they develop new products, enhance existing systems and address evolving technology requirements.
